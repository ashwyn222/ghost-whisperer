Privacy Policy

Ghost Whisperer (package com.live.translate) · Last updated 17 November 2026

Ghost Whisperer is a travel interpreter app made by Ashwin Sharma ("I", "me"). It listens to the person you're talking to, translates what they say into your language, and helps you reply in theirs. This policy explains what the app accesses, why, and where that information goes.

In short: there is no account and no sign-in, and the app has no analytics or advertising. I don't run any server, so I never receive your speech, translations, location or history.

Permissions and data the app uses

DataWhyWhere it goes
Microphone audio To hear the other person and you, so speech can be translated. Passed to your phone's built-in Android speech recognition service (usually Google's). Depending on your device and settings, that service may process audio on your device or on its provider's servers. The app doesn't record or keep audio.
Approximate and precise location To work out which country or region you're in, so the app can pick the local language. Used on your device. Your coordinates are sent to the Android geocoding service (usually Google's) to look up the country and city name. Location is not stored or shared with anyone else.
Recognised text and translations To show and speak translations. Translation runs on your device using Google ML Kit's offline language packs, and the text isn't sent anywhere for translation. Spoken output uses your phone's text-to-speech engine.
Transcript history So you can look back at, replay or copy earlier phrases. Stored only on your phone. You can turn it off in Settings, and it's deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
Settings To remember your language, theme and playback preferences. Stored only on your phone.
Network and audio device status To show whether you're offline and to warn you when earbuds disconnect. Checked on your device only.

Internet use

The app connects to the internet to:

  • download offline language packs (about 30 MB each) from Google;
  • load fonts from Google Fonts;
  • look up place names (geocoding), as described above.

These requests go to Google and are covered by Google's Privacy Policy. Once the packs are downloaded, translation works offline.

What I don't do

  • I don't collect, sell, rent or share personal data.
  • There are no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • I don't create user profiles or ask for your name, email or contacts.

Third-party services

  • Android speech recognition and text-to-speech, as installed on your device
  • Google ML Kit: on-device translation and language identification
  • Android location and geocoding services

These services follow their own privacy policies.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke the microphone and location permissions at any time in Android Settings. Without location, you can choose the language yourself.
  • You can turn transcript history off in the app's Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any information from them.

Security

Your history and settings stay in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date.
